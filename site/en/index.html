About

Shri Sunil Charora is a Rashtriya Lok Dal (RLD) leader from the Jahangirabad–Anupshahr area of Bulandshahr district. He has served on the Bulandshahr Zila Panchayat as the member from Ward No. 50.

He stays close to people on the problems of farmers, young people and everyday village life: at chaupals, at public meetings, and in front of officials with written memorandums.

The people of Anupshahr have always treated me like family. My resolve is that farmers get a fair price for their crops on time, young people get work, women live without fear, and every village moves forward. Whatever your problem, just call or send a WhatsApp message. I stand with you.

Yours, Sunil Charora

Our pledge for Anupshahr

From Anupshahr on the banks of the Ganga to Jahangirabad, Ahar and Aurangabad, we will raise every village's concerns with full strength, from the chaupal all the way to Lucknow. These are not just promises; they are our responsibility.

Prosperity for farmers

  • Full price and on-time payment for sugarcane
  • Enough power for tubewells, at fixed hours
  • Protecting crops from stray cattle
  • Crop insurance and compensation without the runaround

Jobs for young people

  • Skill-training and apprenticeship information in every village
  • Grounds and running tracks for army and police recruitment practice
  • Help getting loans to start your own work

A future for students

  • Push for a degree college and better schooling in the area
  • Help with scholarship forms and documents
  • Village libraries and competitive-exam preparation

Safety and dignity for women

  • The safety of women and girls comes first
  • Earning through self-help groups
  • Widow and old-age pensions on time

Protection from Ganga erosion

  • A fight for permanent embankments and spurs in the Ahar–Anupshahr khadar
  • Fair compensation for farmers hit by floods and erosion

Roads, water, power, healthcare

  • Paved roads to every village
  • Clean drinking water
  • Doctors and medicines at health centres

Questions and answers

Who is Sunil Charora?

Shri Sunil Charora (also written Charaura) is a Rashtriya Lok Dal (RLD) leader from the Jahangirabad–Anupshahr area of Bulandshahr district, Uttar Pradesh, and a former member of the Bulandshahr Zila Panchayat from Ward No. 50.

What number is the Anupshahr Assembly constituency?

Anupshahr (officially also spelt Anoopshahr) is Uttar Pradesh Assembly constituency No. 67. It is in Bulandshahr district and is part of the Bulandshahr Lok Sabha constituency.

Which areas are in the Anupshahr Assembly constituency?

Anupshahr town, Jahangirabad town, Ahar, Aurangabad and many nearby villages are part of this constituency.

How can I contact Sunil Charora?

Call or WhatsApp 97191 66039, or fill in the message form on this website to send your message straight to WhatsApp.

Can I get help with government scheme forms?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp message for help with forms and documents for schemes such as PM-Kisan, the Ayushman card, pensions and scholarships. This is a private public-service initiative, not a government portal.

What is the Rashtriya Lok Dal (RLD)?

The Rashtriya Lok Dal was founded by Chaudhary Ajit Singh. It follows the pro-farmer thinking of former Prime Minister Chaudhary Charan Singh. Its national president is Shri Jayant Chaudhary.
